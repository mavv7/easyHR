# easyHR workflows

## Monthly payroll

1. HR grants an existing workspace member Finance review access in **Workflows → Payroll review**. The reviewer must be a different person from the submitter.
2. In **Attendance**, HR can set the month’s cut-off. Employees submit regular and overtime minutes. The assigned manager or another HR user approves the entries and records the approved overtime amount. The register flags missing submissions and approvals recorded after the cut-off.
3. HR enters or edits drafts in **Payroll**. In **Workflows → Payroll review**, apply scheduled basic pay and recurring allowances or carry them forward from the previous month. A carry-forward excludes last month’s bonuses and overtime. Compensation changes apply from the first day of the selected payroll month.
4. Import approved overtime and review missing drafts, repeated bonuses, and changes from the prior month. Submitting locks the month’s payroll drafts and attendance. Finance can return the run with comments or approve it. Approval snapshots the amounts and publishes a payslip to each included employee.
5. In **Reconciliation**, Finance records payments against the approved run and attaches filing receipts. The register shows employee deductions, employer contributions, remittances, and remaining balances for PAYE, NAPSA, and NHIMA. Filing and payment are performed outside easyHR.

Payroll approval currently supports standard 2026 Zambian monthly payroll using the rules in [Zambia payroll and contracts](zambia-payroll.md). Review employee-specific exceptions before submission.

## Employee records and people tasks

- Employees can see their available leave, approved payslips, reviewed contract records, and contract PDFs in **Workflows → My records**. Payroll questions are linked to the selected month and can be assigned and resolved by HR or Finance.
- HR can attach a PDF to any record in **Contracts**. Uploading a replacement sends the contract back to HR review before the employee can access it again.
- **People tasks** shows contracts that expire in the next 60 days. HR can assign a renewal or probation review to a manager. The manager records an outcome and HR signs it off. HR can schedule a compensation change during sign-off.
- HR can assign individual onboarding or offboarding tasks, or create a checklist for HR, the manager, Finance, and IT. Owners see their tasks and record completion notes. Overview shows in-app alerts for due tasks, upcoming contract endings, and payroll awaiting Finance review.

Contract and receipt attachments support PDFs up to 2 MB. Access uses the workspace role and the specific record.
