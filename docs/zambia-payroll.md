# Zambia payroll and contracts

In Payroll, open a month and enter basic pay and separate totals for taxable cash allowances and bonuses. For 2026, easyHR estimates deductions and net pay for standard monthly employment. It displays the matching employer pension and health contributions alongside the estimate. Drafts retain the pay inputs and recalculate suggestions using the rules for the selected month.

| Item | 2026 monthly calculation | Employer workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ZRA PAYE | 0% on the first K5,100, 20% on the next K2,000, 30% on the next K2,100, then 37% above K9,200. Calculate on taxable pay before pension deductions. | Register with ZRA, withhold PAYE and remit it using the monthly PAYE return ITF/P16 by the 10th of the following month. [ZRA tax information](https://www.zra.org.zm/tax-information/) · [Payment due dates](https://www.zra.org.zm/payment-due-dates/) |
| NAPSA | 5% each for the employee and employer on covered earnings, capped at K37,236 of monthly earnings. Each share is capped at K1,861.80. | Register the employer and eligible workers, submit contribution schedules and remit both shares. [NAPSA 2026 ceiling notice](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/national-pension-scheme-authority_public-notice-revision-in-contribution-ceiling-activity-7417941633180090368-y4Dr) · [Formal sector contributions](https://www.napsa.co.zm/self-service/formal-sector) |
| NHIMA | 1% of monthly basic pay each for the employee and employer. | Register eligible employees and remit both shares. [NHIMA implementation notice](https://www.nhima.co.zm/download/document/0e98ec61c02019110104d23eb0.pdf) |

For example, K12,000 in basic pay plus K3,000 in taxable allowances and bonuses gives K15,000 gross, K3,176 PAYE, K750 employee NAPSA and K120 employee NHIMA. Estimated net pay is K10,954. The employer contributes a further K870.

The calculation assumes the entered cash earnings are taxable for PAYE and covered by NAPSA. Review exemptions, secondary employment, cumulative PAYE, irregular payments and termination pay separately. Employer levies, filings and remittances are outside the draft calculation. [ZRA employer PAYE guide](https://www.zra.org.zm/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/EMPLOYERS-GUIDE-TO-PAY-AS-YOU-EARN-2020.pdf)

## Contracts

Use Company Settings to record the legal employer name, PACRA registration number and official address shown in the contract register. PACRA maintains the register of companies and business names and offers a Legal Entity Printout to check registration details. [PACRA Business Search](https://search.pacra.org.zm/) · [PACRA Legal Entity Printout](https://info.pacra.org.zm/how-do-i-view-the-details-of-my-company/)

Use the contract's basic pay and payment terms fields to specify monthly wages, how allowances and bonuses are calculated, any benefits in cash or in kind, and the payment interval. The Employment Code Act's Second Schedule lists the remaining particulars for the written agreement. [Employment Code Act, 2019](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/node/7948)
